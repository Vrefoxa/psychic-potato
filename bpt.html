🛡 Hardening

A static page can't be literally unbreakable — but the realistic vectors are closed.

  • Target content never executes. Everything from the target is escaped and written as text; script tags never run through innerHTML.
  • AI output is sandboxed. The report renders in an iframe with sandbox and no allow-scripts — a malicious completion cannot touch this page or your key.
  • Strict CSP. default-src 'none', no objects, no forms, no base-uri hijack, connect-src https: only.
  • No credentials to proxies. Fetches use credentials:"omit" and strip the Referer.
  • Regex budgets. Bodies capped at 400 KB, secret scanning capped at 200 KB with hard match counters — no ReDoS hang.
  • Watchdog. Any scan running past 6 minutes is aborted automatically, along with dedupe and a 400-finding ceiling.
  • Scheme allowlist. Only http/https accepted; credentials stripped; fragments dropped.
  • Key hygiene. Never logged, masked in the field, optional session-only storage.
  • No server. Exports are generated locally as blobs; nothing is uploaded anywhere.
